How to Get Involved

Three simple steps to find your community at GIC.

🙋
1. Show Up
Visit any of our campuses this Sunday. Our welcome team will connect you with the right ministry from the moment you walk in.
💬
2. Connect
Fill out a connection card, reach out via the contact form, or call our office and we'll personally walk you through joining a ministry.
🌱
3. Grow
Jump in, build friendships, serve others, and watch God transform your life through community, accountability, and purpose.
Women's Ministry
V.O.G.U.E. Women's Ministry

V.O.G.U.E. stands for Victorious, Outstanding, Gracious, Unctuous & Excellent — a ministry designed by God for the modern woman of faith. We exist to empower women to walk boldly in their identity, gifts, and purpose.

Through conferences, fellowships, mentorship, and discipleship, VOGUE builds a sisterhood where women are celebrated, supported, and challenged to go deeper in their walk with God.

🌸Monthly fellowship gatherings & conferences
📖Women's Bible study & discipleship groups
🤝Mentorship & personal development programs
🌍Open to women at all GIC campus locations
Join VOGUE Ministry
Men's Ministry
Men of Valor

The Grace Int'l Church Men's Ministry — Men of Valor — is for men, by men. We exist to support and address the issues specific to men in today's world, through the lens of Scripture.

Men of Valor Internationally jumpstarts men's discipleship through conferences, emphasizing biblical principles and practices. We host programs for spiritual empowerment, marital enrichment, financial empowerment, and so much more.

Come experience acceptance, mentorship, fellowship, and life transformation through the Word of God in an open, loving, and accountable environment.

💪Spiritual empowerment & biblical discipleship
💍Marital enrichment & family leadership
💰Financial stewardship & empowerment
🤜Brotherhood, accountability & mentorship
Join Men of Valor
🔥 Youth Ministry
One Way Youth Ministry

One Way Youth Ministry is where the next generation finds their faith, their tribe, and their purpose. We're passionate about raising young people who are on fire for God and rooted in truth.

Through weekly services, retreats, community outreach, worship nights, and mentorship, we create a safe space where young people can ask real questions, encounter God authentically, and grow together.

🎮Fun, engaging weekly youth services
🏕Youth retreats & camping experiences
🎤Worship nights & prayer events
🤝Community service & outreach projects

Children's Ministry
Grace Children

Grace Children's Ministry is committed to nurturing young hearts from ages 2–12 in the love, truth, and grace of God. We believe that a child's spiritual foundation is one of the most important investments a family can make.

Every Sunday, our trained and caring team creates age-appropriate, safe, and fun environments where children encounter God's love through Bible stories, worship, games, and creative activities.

👶Faith-building activities for children ages 2–12
📚Bible-based curriculum for all age groups
🔒Safe, screened & trained volunteers
🎨Creative worship, games & storytelling

Worship Ministry
Choir & Music Team

The GIC Choir & Music Team is the heartbeat of our worship experience. We believe that music is a powerful vehicle for encountering the presence of God, and we take that calling seriously.

Whether you're a vocalist, instrumentalist, sound engineer, or creative — if you have a gift and a heart for worship, we want you on the team. We rehearse, we grow, and we lead God's people into His presence every Sunday.

🎤Choir singers & lead vocalists
🎹Instrumentalists — keys, guitar, drums, bass
🎧Sound engineers & media team
📅Weekly rehearsals & Sunday services
